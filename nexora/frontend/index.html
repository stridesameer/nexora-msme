CSV format

Required: transaction_id, transaction_date, description, transaction_type, amount

Optional: category, due_date, expected_payment_date, actual_payment_date, loan_repayment_due_date, opening_balance, linked_invoice_id

transaction_type: income, expense (cash already moved) · invoice (customer owes you) · bill (you owe a supplier) · loan_repayment. Invoices, bills and loans count as cash only once actual_payment_date is filled in. Amounts are positive; the type sets the direction.
